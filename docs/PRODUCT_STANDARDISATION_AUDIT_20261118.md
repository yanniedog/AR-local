## Implemented contract

- A separate generation-bound criteria catalogue carries comparable typed
  eligibility, constraints, fees, conditions, bundles and features. Original
  rates, tiers, raw details and complete normalized facts retain their existing
  evidence archives. Exact decimal strings, units, fee cadence, qualifiers,
  effective dates and source pointers are retained. Conditional and unknown
  values cannot silently become unconditional filter matches.
- Every captured ingest has durable document-discovery obligations. Declared
  product sources, reviewed adapter candidates and exact official-directory
  mappings are distinct from acquired, applicable product documents. Provider
  homepages are navigation evidence and never verified legal proof. Scoped
  citations survive shared-document compaction.
- Discovery, acquisition, extraction and optional publication fail independently
  of financial publication. Financial capture still preserves bounded raw
  evidence; optional failures retain diagnostics and retry obligations.
- App transport verifies complete index/shard inventories, hashes, source
  identity and budgets before admitting a catalogue. Filtering uses explicit
  match/no-match/unknown outcomes. Evidence actions require actual pointer
  association; a generic overview does not substitute for a fact citation.
- OCR is disabled by default. Its optional adapter requires explicitly pinned
  tools and guarded execution. Missing OCR remains unavailable/pending.

## Capture timeout mitigation and remaining proof

The November16 handoff records `complete_terms_capture_exceeds_safe_bounds`
with raw stages preserved. Capture now reuses one parameter registry context
and acquisition queue per invocation. An exact-version acquisition-check index
removes a repeated full-history scan and sort. Its optional installation permits
100ms lock waits and five seconds of SQLite VM work, restores the original
connection limits and logs interruption without invalidating accepted evidence.
The product/byte/deadline limits and immutable receipt rules remain unchanged.

Two separate fresh Windows stores captured all 1618 retained May19 products
(18,744,763 original bytes): before66.859s, after67.156s under profiling. This
is no demonstrated first-day speed improvement. Across their1534 actual raw
versions, exact-check lookups took0.172s before versus0.016s after; query plans
changed from SCAN plus temporary sort to the exact-version index. These are
local historical measurements, not accumulated-history/current3596/Pi proof.
The live timeout remains unresolved until a bounded actual retry succeeds;
retain its raw stage and do not raise limits or infer release qualification.
